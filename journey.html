Career journey

From specialty pharmacy and patient care to PBM technology and AI governance.

Career timeline

  1. 2010 to 2013

    Patient Access

    Pharmacy Care Coordinator

    US Bioservices Specialty Pharmacy (AmerisourceBergen), Frisco, TX

    • Coordinated specialty therapy access among patients, prescribers, pharmacies, and payers, including copay assistance, foundation funding, free-drug programs, and manufacturer support enrollment
    • Kept REMS documentation and authorization requirements on schedule; documented adverse drug reactions and completed clinical follow-up

  2. 2017

    Licensure

    Doctor of Pharmacy, magna cum laude

    Midwestern University College of Pharmacy, Glendale, AZ

    • Rho Chi Honor Society
    • Registered Pharmacist, California State Board of Pharmacy

  3. 2017 to 2024

    Patient Care

    Staff Pharmacist

    Walmart, Fresno, CA (full-time 2 years, per diem 5 years)

    • Supervised and trained pharmacy technicians and associates in a high-volume retail pharmacy
    • Verified prescriptions; provided counseling, immunizations, and medication therapy support
    • Improved inventory controls to reduce medication waste

  4. 2019 to 2021

    Formulary and Automation

    Clinical Formulary Pharmacist

    Envolve Pharmacy Solutions, Fresno, CA

    • Built and maintained SmartPA automated prior authorization and DUR edits, improving adjudication accuracy and formulary enforcement
    • Converted formulary changes into configurable system logic and tested it before release
    • 2020 Key Contributor Award for exceeding performance targets on PBM initiatives

  5. 2021 to 2022

    Health Plan

    Clinical Pharmacist

    Central California Alliance for Health, remote

    • Reviewed pharmacy and medical prior authorization requests, including physician-administered drugs
    • Audited PBM vendor policies and performance as part of delegation oversight
    • Analyzed pharmacy claims and utilization to inform benefit decisions

  6. 2022 to 2024

    PBM Consulting

    Pharmaceutical Services Consultant, Clinical Pharmacist

    Conduent, remote

    • Wrote pharmacy policy, prior authorization criteria, and drug evaluations for DUR programs
    • Led system demonstrations and clinical validation during SIT; trained client staff on PA automation
    • Resolved pharmacy data issues with Medi-Span and First Databank; shaped data governance practices

  7. 2024 to 2026

    Product and Technology

    Technology Solutions Management Consultant, PBM; Clinical Pharmacist

    Conduent Government Healthcare Solutions, state Medicaid PBM programs

    • Advised IT leadership on PBM roadmap and feature priority
    • Converted NCPDP Telecommunication F6, SCRIPT, ePA, and Formulary & Benefit changes into requirements and solution designs
    • Wrote requirements for AI-supported initiatives, including escalation paths and human review
    • Aligned technical scope and timelines in RFP responses

  8. 2025

    AI Governance

    URAC Health Care AI Accreditation Advisory Committee

    Advisory member since April 2025

    • Part of the first working group developing national accreditation standards for AI in healthcare

  9. Dec 2025

    Federal Initiative

    CMS IDea Challenge: Strategies for Securing Member IDs

    Selected participant, in-person event, San Francisco, CA, December 5, 2025

    • Worked with teams from mixed backgrounds on ideas to strengthen the security of health plan member IDs and reduce fraud
    • Part of the CMS Crushing Fraud, Waste, and Abuse initiative

    Event summary (PDF)
